# Exam goal estimate (LP-23)

`POST /api/v1/learning/exam-plan/estimate` estimates one missing value among `scopeContents`, `dailyMinutes`, and `targetPercent` from the other two. It does not save a plan or claim a probability of passing an exam. The response shows a baseline and a more cautious range with suggested changes if the requested goal is outside the modeled capacity.

The scope is a set of distinct **learning content IDs**, not a count of questions or repeated clicks. The selection is checked against the authenticated account's own accessible learning overview. A fixed scope must not exceed the selected contents. If scope is the unknown, the result is capped at the number of available selected contents. Only historical attempts on those contents count as already mastered, and only if the latest uninterrupted sequence has three confident correct answers separated by at least one and then three days. Guesses, wrong answers and viewed explanations reset the sequence. Existing mastery is therefore a stricter estimate than the general progress overview and is never inferred from time spent or a number of questions.

For each remaining content, the capacity model reserves 20 minutes for the introduction and first answer, then five minutes for each of two due reviews (earliest day 1 and day 4). Due work takes priority over new work. It reserves 20% of each day's capacity for interruptions, or 40% in the cautious scenario. Listed school weekdays halve daily capacity; break dates have zero capacity. The daily limit always applies. The optional exam date is exclusive (no studying on exam day); without one, `horizonDays` defaults to 28. Inputs are bounded to 365 days, 480 minutes per day, and 2,000 contents.

A result is a planning estimate, not an exam pass prediction. Difficulty, retention and individual pace vary; the lower range communicates some of this uncertainty without pretending to be a calibrated statistical confidence interval. If the goal is outside capacity, the response suggests a later date (within one year), a feasible daily time if under the limit, a smaller scope, or a lower target. The UI presents the assumptions next to the result. Plans are recalculated on demand as content and learning history change.
